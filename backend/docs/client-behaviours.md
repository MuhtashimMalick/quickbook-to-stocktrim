# Behaviours to tell the client

- Non-inventory BOM components (services, other charges) are excluded from the BOM (A12, to be confirmed with the client).
- A sub-assembly without a BOM of its own appears in StockTrim as a purchased item.
- Negative stock (A13): sent as-is. StockTrim stores negative stock on hand unchanged (verified on the TEST account: probe and the first real push read-back); the 134 negative items appear in StockTrim with their negative quantity. If a future StockTrim version rejects negatives, the engine retries those items with 0 and reports their ListIDs.
- Supplier city and phone cannot be transferred (the StockTrim supplier has no such fields): 333 of 382 suppliers have a city and 289 a phone (6 Nov pull).
- Values cleared in QuickBooks, by field (probed on the TEST account, 2026-11-17): CAN be cleared in StockTrim (we send an explicit empty string): supplier email, contact, address lines, state, post code, country; product category. CANNOT be cleared (we keep omitting them, the old StockTrim value stays): product cost, price, minimum and maximum shelf level (StockTrim only accepts `0`, which is a real value that changes planning), the product's preferred-supplier link (clearing `supplierCode` leaves the supplier list entry behind), and the supplier external id (always set). Customers are the opposite of products: a customer PUT replaces the whole record, so a cleared email/phone/address IS cleared. Note: a product with no QuickBooks category now clears a category curated by hand in StockTrim (needs the client's decision, see the category note below).
- Categories come from the top-level QuickBooks group. A category set in QuickBooks can never be cleared, and it will overwrite categories curated in StockTrim: needs a client decision before production.
- Duplicate item names (the product key is the QuickBooks `Name`, case-insensitive; inventory and assembly items share it): the client moved old items into `Obsolete Inventory:` / `Obsolete Assemblies:` (inactive) and reused the same part numbers for current items, so 18 names have two items. Rule per group of items with the same name: exactly ONE active item -> it is sent, the inactive twins are set aside (not sent, pre-flight WARNING `duplicate_key_set_aside`); NO active item -> all are set aside (warning only, nothing is sent for that name); TWO OR MORE active -> all are held back and the pre-flight BLOCKS (`qb_products_quarantined`): rename one in QuickBooks. Order lines and BOM lines that point at a set-aside item are skipped (sales/purchase order lines count as `non_product`, BOM lines as `bom_component_excluded`); they are never moved to the active item with the same name, because the sold or built item may really have been the old one. Inactive vendors never block an active vendor with the same name.
- Inactive records (suppliers, customers) are not pushed.
- BOM quantities are stored by StockTrim with 5 decimals, rounded half-up (0.123456 becomes 0.12346); we round the same way before sending (summing repeated components first), so a QuickBooks quantity with more than 5 decimals shows rounded, and one below 0.000005 (which StockTrim would store as 0) is not sent and is reported as a warning.
- Customers cannot be deleted through the StockTrim API: every customer we push (13,588 on the first full load, plus the probe customer `zz-probe-cust`) can only be removed in the StockTrim UI. Customer codes are the QuickBooks ListIDs, matched case-insensitively.
- Cost, price and stock on hand are stored by StockTrim with 2 decimals, rounded half-up (1.005 becomes 1.01); we round the same way before sending, so a QuickBooks value with more decimals (e.g. a 3-decimal cost) will show rounded in StockTrim. For negative stock the tie rule (-1.005 -> -1.01) is assumed, not verified.
- Incremental sync pushes only changed records (compared by payload hash). A customer PUT replaces the whole StockTrim record, so only customers that changed in QuickBooks overwrite hand edits made in StockTrim; unchanged customers are never touched, with these exceptions, each of which re-sends customers and so overwrites hand edits made in StockTrim: the first sync after migration `0005` unless the baseline was stamped, a re-queue after a StockTrim wipe, an explicit `mode=initial` customers run, and any mapper or DTO change that alters every customer's hash (about 13,600 PUTs, roughly 70 minutes). Records that disappear from QuickBooks are reported, never deleted in StockTrim.


## Sales orders and purchase orders: defaults the client may change
- Sales history: only orders dated within the last 3 years are sent (`SALES_HISTORY_YEARS`).
- Quantity: the ORDERED quantity of each sales-order line; for a manually closed sales order the INVOICED quantity (closing cancels the unshipped remainder; a closed line with nothing invoiced is skipped).
- Skipped sales-order lines: items that are not pushed products (shipping, discounts, fees, non-inventory, unknown), lines inside bundles/groups, and lines with a zero or negative quantity. They are counted in the dry-run.
- Purchase orders: ALL of them are sent, with the status taken from QuickBooks: open -> Sent (remaining quantity = ordered - received), fully received -> Received (ordered quantity), manually closed but not fully received -> Received (so StockTrim does not count it as on order). A PO whose state changes later is re-sent with the new status. POs whose vendor is not a pushed supplier, or with no pushable line, are skipped.
- No product-level "stock on order" is sent: StockTrim derives it from the purchase orders.
- Units of measure are not converted: quantities go to StockTrim exactly as QuickBooks holds them.
- Sales-order customers: an order is linked to its customer only when that customer was pushed (inactive customers are not): their orders are sent without a customer.
- Purchase orders that share a RefNumber in QuickBooks (10 pairs today) are merged into ONE purchase order by StockTrim (lines united); the expected date (`ExpectedDate`) is accepted by StockTrim but not stored on the PO lines.
- StockTrim merges purchase orders that share a referenceNumber (and does not store the PO line expected date `etaDate`: to raise with Neil). QuickBooks allows duplicate RefNumbers (10 pairs today), so we send those as `RefNumber-TransactionNumber`; unique RefNumbers are sent unchanged.
